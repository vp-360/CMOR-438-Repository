These are the datasets used in the multilayer perceptron (neural network) notebook.

## MNIST (handwritten digits)

Handwritten digits 0 through 9, the classic benchmark for image classification.

- Source: http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/
- Overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MNIST_database

## Fashion-MNIST (clothing)

Ten categories of clothing (T-shirt/top, trouser, pullover, dress, coat, sandal, shirt, sneaker, bag, ankle boot). A drop-in replacement for MNIST with the same shape, but harder to classify.

- Source: https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist
